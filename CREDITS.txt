Block Z — third-party credits
================================

Map art
-------
Arena ground painting generated for Block Z v20, based on a layout
reference provided by Eric Montes. Trees, ruins, crates and barrels
are drawn as in-game sprites / boxes on top of that painting.

GORGON sprites are original artwork generated for Block Z (no third-party
characters, names or logos).

The in-game 8-voice pooled playback system and the iOS "Toca para empezar"
audio unlock are original to Block Z. No procedural synth beeps remain.
v20.1 checked the baked SFX for chiptune-like spectra (very peaky, thin
high-frequency buzz); the hero attacks above are recorded sources.

v20.2.1 additions — Round 10: VORGATH in the lost hell planet (renamed/redesigned in v20.6)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Final boss theme (v20.2.3): "Final Confrontation" (track 19, "The Nine Lives or Er the Cat" OST)
  Author: tcarisland
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/final-confrontation
  License: CC-BY 4.0
  File: assets/mus/abismo.mp3 (trimmed 4 s-156 s for the loop, loudness-normalised, 112 kbps)
  Dramatic orchestral boss music with timpani, low brass and choir. Not chiptune.

Vorgath voice line "Este mundo... ya es mío." (v20.6): Microsoft Edge neural TTS
  (es-ES-AlvaroNeural), pitched down with formant preservation, a sub-octave layer and
  a dark echo. File: assets/sfx/villain_voice.mp3

Abyss deep impact: assets/sfx/abyss_boom.mp3 — made in-house by pitching
  down and layering the CC0 samples already credited above (explode.mp3,
  stomp.mp3) with a generated reverb.

VORGATH sprites (assets/spr/vorgath_walk_f/cast_f/snap_f.webp), the flying boss ÍCARO
(assets/spr/icaro_f.webp) and the hell-planet ground painting are original artwork
generated for Block Z and keyed through the chroma-key pipeline (v20.6 redesign: no
third-party characters, names, costumes or logos).
The mega-powers ("MAZO DE TORMENTA", "ARMADURA TITÁN", "FURIA SALVAJE") are original
names with original procedural designs; no third-party logos.
